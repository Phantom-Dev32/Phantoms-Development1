About Us

Phantoms Development is an independent development company founded in 2024, focused on creating high-quality digital products, development solutions, and game-related services.

What started as a small two-person development team has continued to grow and evolve. Through experience, collaboration and a commitment to improving our work, we have expanded beyond game assets and FiveM development into a wider range of digital services.

Our services and projects now include FiveM development, game assets, websites, Discord bots, software solutions and other custom development projects.

Our Development Team

One of our developers established Grower Development, an independent development group that continues to work alongside Phantoms Development.

Both groups operate under their own names and maintain individual focuses while collaborating as a development team. Grower Development primarily focuses on FiveM development and related projects, while Phantoms continues expanding into websites, Discord solutions, software, digital services and other areas.

Looking Ahead

Our vision is to build Phantoms Development into a broader digital development company capable of delivering a diverse range of products and services.

We are committed to continually improving our work, expanding our expertise and taking on new challenges as technology and client needs evolve.

Whether we're developing a FiveM resource, building a website, creating a Discord bot or working on a completely new project, our focus remains the same: creating reliable, high-quality digital solutions while continuing to grow as a development team.
